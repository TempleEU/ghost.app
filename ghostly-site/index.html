What Ghostly is

A privacy-focused messenger that puts you in control

Ghostly is an independent Android messaging application focused on privacy, security and user control. It builds on the proven Signal/Molly architecture while maintaining its own Ghostly branding, configuration and privacy enhancements.

Ghostly is not affiliated with Signal and does not claim to be.
